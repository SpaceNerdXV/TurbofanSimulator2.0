02 · Theory

How a turbofan works

A turbofan pairs a Brayton-cycle gas-generator core with a large, ducted front fan. Incoming air splits into two concentric streams: a cold high-mass bypass flow and a hot core flow.

  1. 0Intake
  3. 2Fan & Bypass
  5. 25Compressors
  7. 3Combustor

  11. 7Dual Nozzles

Process 1 · Intake & Fan

Capture & Split

Air passes through the laminar inlet lip and wide-chord front fan, splitting into an outer cold bypass duct and an inner core stream at the splitter lip.

BPR = ṁbypass / ṁcore

Process 2 · Compression

Squeeze

Multi-stage LP booster and HP axial compressors raise core pressure up to 60:1, preparing dense, high-temperature air for efficient combustion.

Tt3 = Tt25 · π(γ−1)/γ

Process 3 · Combustion

Bang

Atomized Jet-A burns continuously at near-constant pressure inside an annular combustor cooled by film air, raising gas temperature up to 2,150 K.

Tt4 ≈ 1500–2150 K

Process 4 · Turbine & Exhaust

Drive & Propel

The HPT drives the core; the LPT extracts deeper energy to spin the massive front fan, generating 75–85% of thrust from cold bypass air.

F = ṁc(Vc−V0) + ṁb(Vb−V0)

Why high-bypass turbofans are dramatically more efficient

Thrust equals mass flow times velocity change (F = ṁ · ΔV), while wasted exhaust kinetic energy scales with ΔV². By using a multi-stage Low-Pressure Turbine to drive a huge front fan, a high-bypass turbofan accelerates a massive column of bypass air by a moderate velocity increment — slashing fuel burn (TSFC) and acoustic noise compared to a pure turbojet.

PHPT = PHPC + Pacc  ·  PLPT = PFan + PLPC

05 · Study notes

Turbofan glossary

Key aerospace propulsion metrics displayed on the EICAS panel.

N1 / N2 / N3

Low-, high-, and intermediate-pressure concentric spool speeds as a percentage of design RPM.

Bypass Ratio (BPR)

Ratio of cold air mass flow passing around the core to hot air passing through the core.

EGT

Exhaust gas temperature measured downstream of the turbine to protect blade alloys from creep.

OPR

Overall pressure ratio — total pressure at HPC discharge divided by inlet total pressure.

TSFC

Thrust-specific fuel consumption in grams of fuel burned per kilonewton-second of thrust.

EPR

Engine pressure ratio — turbine discharge total pressure divided by compressor inlet pressure.

Cascade Reverser

Translating nacelle sleeve and blocker doors that redirect cold bypass air forward upon landing.

FADEC

Full Authority Digital Engine Control governing fuel scheduling, ignition, and redline limits.

Chapter 01 · Cockpit Procedures

FADEC Engine Control & Start Sequence

A turbofan cannot start simply by turning on fuel—without compressed airflow from a spinning compressor, fuel would pool and cause a destructive hot-start fire. Follow this exact 5-step sequence (or press AUTO-START):

Step 1 · Electrical Power

FADEC Bus → ONLINE

Energizes the dual-channel Full Authority Digital Engine Control computers, fuel metering valves, EICAS sensors, and cockpit annunciators.

Interlock: Master Bus required for all valves

Step 2 · Pneumatic Motoring

Air Starter → ENGAGED

High-pressure bleed air spins the HP core shaft (N2) up toward ~24.5% RPM, drawing fresh air through the compressors and combustor before fuel enters.

Target: N2 ≥ 15.0% before light-off

Step 3 · High-Energy Spark

Igniters → ARMED

Fires dual capacitive-discharge igniter plugs inside the annular combustor dome (audible as crisp electrical snaps when Audio is enabled).

Exciter output: ~20,000 V pulsed arc

Step 4 · Light-Off & Acceleration

Fuel Valve → RUN (at ≥15% N2)

Opens the high-pressure fuel shutoff valve. Atomized Jet-A sprays into the combustor, ignites, and accelerates N2 to self-sustaining idle (~60% N2, ~25% N1), automatically disengaging the starter.

Watch: EGT peaks briefly then stabilizes

Step 5 · Thrust Lever Angle (TLA)

IDLE → CRUISE → CLIMB → TO/GA

Schedules fuel flow from ground idle up to 100% Takeoff/Go-Around power. On fighter engines with an Afterburner Module (F135, F119, EJ200, AL-31F), advancing TLA to 120% MAX A/B ignites the augmentor rings.

Cruise (~72% TLA) yields lowest TSFC

Step 6 · Shutdown & Cold Reset

SHUTDOWN vs. MASTER RESET

SHUTDOWN closes the fuel valve while keeping FADEC powered so you can monitor EGT cooling and spool coast-down. MASTER RESET instantly returns all rotors and temperatures to cold static.

Dry motoring: Starter ON + Fuel CUTOFF

Chapter 02 · Aerodynamic Braking

How the Cascade Thrust Reverser Blocks & Reverses Airflow

On high-bypass commercial turbofans, 75–85% of total thrust comes from the cold bypass duct. Upon touchdown, deploying REV THRUST redirects that massive bypass stream forward without reversing rotor spin direction.

Part A · Outer Cowling

Translating Cowl Sleeve

Hydraulic actuators slide the aft outer nacelle sleeve rearward along axial tracks, uncovering a circumferential ring of turning vanes.

Travel: Slides aft to expose cascade bay

Part B · Internal Seal

18 Pivoting Blocker Doors

Linked by mechanical drag struts to the core cowl, 18 overlapping titanium blocker doors swing radially inward across the bypass annulus, sealing against the core wall to physically block rearward cold airflow.

Seals 100% of cold bypass duct exit

Part C · Flow Deflection

Cascade Turning Vanes

Blocked high-pressure fan air escapes radially outward through curved cascade louvers that turn the jet ~38° forward, creating strong negative net thrust (-kN) to decelerate the aircraft.

Visualized by forward cyan streams & plumes

Part D · Core Independence

Hot Core Continues Aft

Because the blocker doors only seal the outer bypass annulus, the inner gas-generator core continues exhausting cleanly out the rear nozzle so the turbine never stalls or overheats.

Available on engines with BPR > 0.25

Chapter 03 · 3D Cutaway Anatomy

Every Station & Mechanical Assembly Explained

Hover over any part in the 3D viewport with Inspect: On to highlight it in cyan and read live telemetry. Here is what every assembly does inside the engine:

Station 0–1 · Forward Intake

Laminar Lip & Ogive Spinner

The polished aluminum intake lip slows incoming air smoothly to Mach ~0.5 before the fan face, while the conical spinner with its white spiral marker sheds ice centrifugally away from the core.

Station 2 · Primary Propulsor

Swept Composite Fan Rotor

3D-twisted carbon-fiber blades with titanium leading edges act like a ducted propeller, accelerating huge volumes of air into both the outer bypass duct and inner core splitter.

Station 13 · Cold Stream

Bypass Duct & Outlet Guide Vanes

24 radial Outlet Guide Vanes (OGVs) behind the fan straighten swirling air into pure axial momentum, maximizing forward thrust and reducing acoustic fan noise.

Station 23–3 · Compression

LPC Booster & Multi-Stage HPC

Alternating rows of spinning rotor blades and stationary stator vanes squeeze core air through a narrowing annulus up to 60:1 pressure ratio, heating air by adiabatic compression.

Station 4 · Heat Addition

Double-Annular Swirl Combustor

16 air-blast fuel nozzles atomize Jet-A into a continuous toroidal flame stabilized by swirlers, while dilution air cools the liner and tailors the temperature profile entering the HPT.

Station 41–48 · Power Extraction

HPT & LPT Turbine Stages

Superalloy single-crystal blades glow cherry-orange as hot gas expands across them. The HPT drives the high-speed core compressor; the LPT drives the large front fan.

Drive Train · Concentric Shafts

1-, 2-, and 3-Spool Shafts

Color-coded concentric shafts run down the centerline: Cyan (N1 LP) connects LPT to Fan; Amber (N2 HP) connects HPT to HPC; Emerald (N3 IP) appears in 3-spool Rolls-Royce architectures.

Accessories & Frame

Bearing Sumps & Chin Gearbox

Structural frame struts support roller bearings carrying axial thrust loads, while a vertical tower shaft drives the bottom-mounted Accessory Gearbox (fuel, oil, and hydraulic pumps).

Station 6–8 · Exhaust System

Mixer, Chevrons, Plug & Con-Di Nozzle

Low-bypass engines use a lobed daisy mixer to blend hot and cold streams; high-bypass nacelles use acoustic chevrons; fighter engines add flameholder rings and variable-area nozzle petals.

Chapter 05 · Instrumentation & Visualizers

Reading the EICAS Gauges & 3D Airflow Streams

The right-hand EICAS panel updates at 60 Hz from the portable TurbofanPhysicsEngine solver.

Spool Tachometers

N1, N2 & N3 (% RPM)

N1 measures the front fan / LP spool (primary thrust indicator). N2 measures the high-pressure core spool. N3 activates when 3-Spool architecture is selected.

Thermal Limit

EGT (°C) & Redline

Exhaust Gas Temperature downstream of the turbine. Turns amber above 88% of redline and flashes red if turbine alloy limits are exceeded.

Efficiency Metrics

TSFC & EPR

TSFC (g/kN·s) measures fuel burned per unit thrust—lower is more efficient. EPR (P5/P2) compares turbine exit pressure to intake pressure.

3D Flow Visualization

Color-Coded Streamlines

Cyan streams show cold intake and bypass air; forward-deflected cyan streams show reversed cascade flow; orange-gold streams show ignited core exhaust.
